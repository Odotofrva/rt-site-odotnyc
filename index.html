About RoboTechi

Technology, culture, and creative engineering in one ecosystem.

Technology division within the OdotNYC ecosystem.

Built at the intersection of hardware, software, and culture, RoboTechi is dedicated to shaping the next generation of builders, innovators, and digital creators.

At its core, RoboTechi explores modern embedded systems, IoT development, custom hardware concepts, and experimental interfaces — transforming ideas into tangible, functional technology.

  • Electronics fundamentals and maker education
  • Embedded systems, IoT, and prototype workflows
  • Creative web, AR, and product storytelling
